# Troubleshooting Tips

- If your motherboard manual does not include a speaker beep code table, check the motherboard specifications to find out what type of BIOS it uses (such as AMI, Award or Phoenix). Once you know the BIOS type, search online for the corresponding beep code table.
- Beep codes are helpful diagnostic signals from the motherboard, but they aren't always definitive. For example, in AMI BIOS, 1 long beep and 2 short beeps usually point to a GPU error. However, it could also be caused by something as simple as a loose monitor power connector. Always double-check related components and connections before assuming a specific hardware failure.
- If the PC shows no video, clean the RAM contacts with a bulb air blower or isopropyl alcohol. Some people use an eraser (the "eraser trick"), but using compressed air or isopropyl alcohol is safer.
